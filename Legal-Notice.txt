3. AI Features: What to Know
--------------------------------------------------------------------
  1. AI answers are generated by a language model and may be
     inaccurate, incomplete or out of date. They are an aid for finding
     your own material, and do not constitute legal, medical,
     financial or any other professional advice. Do not rely on them as
     the sole basis for a decision. Verify against the original material.

  2. Citation markers ([1] [2]) point to the retrieved passages so you
     can check the source, but the model's restatement of a passage can
     still be inaccurate.

  3. AI conversation requires a model. It can be an Ollama model
     installed on your machine, or a cloud AI service you configure
     yourself. The Software does not provide any AI model, and does not
     proxy or relay any AI service.

  4. When you use a cloud AI, the content needed for an answer is handled
     by the provider you configured, under that provider's own terms
     and privacy policy. Satisfy yourself that you trust it.


4. Privacy
--------------------------------------------------------------------
  1. The Software does not collect or transmit your material. It contains
     no usage statistics, no crash reporting and no telemetry of any kind.

  2. Your material is stored in the library folder you choose, and the
     search index is likewise kept on your own machine.

  3. Data leaves your machine only in the following cases, each triggered
     by you:

       - when you capture a web page, an internet connection is used to
         read that page;
       - when you have configured a cloud AI and ask a question, the
         content needed for the answer is sent to the provider you chose;
       - when you export a backup and place the file elsewhere yourself.

  4. Uninstalling the Software does not delete your material. To erase it,
     choose the permanent-delete option in the uninstaller, or remove the
     library folder yourself.

  5. The Software requires no account registration and asks for no
     personal identification. An activation code is used only to verify
     licensing and contains no personal data about you.
