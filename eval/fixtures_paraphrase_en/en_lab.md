# Materials Testing Laboratory — Equipment Ledger

## Instrument Ledger
The universal testing machine WDW-300 is managed by Ma Li. All three machines share a calibration cycle of 12 months.

## Calibration Bodies
Mechanical instruments are calibrated by the Provincial Institute of Metrology.

## Nonconformity Handling
If a machine fails calibration it is taken out of service at once and a tag is posted; the equipment administrator then seals it.
