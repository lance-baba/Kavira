# Flood Control Operation Record — Qinghe Reservoir

## Rainfall Event
From 8 to 10 June a heavy rainfall event delivered a cumulative rainfall of 210 millimetres.

## Inflow Process
On 11 June the reservoir inflow peaked at 3200 cubic metres per second.

## Spillway Operation
On 12 June the reservoir opened three gates to release floodwater.

## Emergency Duties
Urban drainage was assigned to the Drainage Management Office.
