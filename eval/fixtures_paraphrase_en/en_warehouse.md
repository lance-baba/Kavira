# Annual Inventory Audit — North Warehouse

## Audit Scope
The audit covered warehouse A and warehouse B. A total of 128 stock items were counted.

## Warehouse A
Item A-1001, a hexagonal bolt of size M12, had a book quantity of 2400 and a physical count of 2396, a shortage of 4 pieces.

## Handling of Shortages
Item A-1001 was written off as normal wear. Items A-2103 and B-3302 were scheduled for recheck.

## Sign-off
