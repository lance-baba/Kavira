# Excavation Monitoring Plan — Metro Line 4 Station

## Project Overview
The foundation pit is about 186 metres long and the excavation depth is about 17.6 metres.

## Monitoring Items
Monitored quantities include wall top horizontal displacement, wall top vertical displacement, inclinometer, strut axial force, ground surface settlement outside the pit, and groundwater level outside the pit.

## Monitoring Frequency
During excavation the readings are taken once per day.

## Alarm Values
The cumulative alarm value for wall top horizontal displacement is 24mm and the rate alarm value is 2mm per day.
