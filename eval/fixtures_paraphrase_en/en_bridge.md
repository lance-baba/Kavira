# Bridge Inspection Report — Riverside Viaduct

## Overview
The Riverside Viaduct carries the eastern ring road across the river. Construction started in 2009 and the bridge opened to traffic in 2011. The total length is 1240 metres, and the designed service life is 100 years.

## Expansion Joints
Four expansion joints run along the deck, numbered SS-01 to SS-04 from north to south. Each joint permits thermal movement of up to 120 millimetres.

## Recorded Defects
Defect ZZ-07 was logged on the central span. The bearing pad had developed shear deformation, and the cause was traced to temperature variation during the summer months.

## Inspection Equipment
Surface cracks are measured with the crack width gauge ZBL-F120. Its calibration certificate is valid until 2026-12-05.
